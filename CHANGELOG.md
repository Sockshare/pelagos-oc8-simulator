# Changelog

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-14

### Added
- Initial release of Pelagos OC-8 & 18-DOF Hexapod 3D Teardown Field Simulator.
- Nine procedural biomimetic robotic units (`OC-8`, `HX-18`, `SC-8`, `HB-7`, `CN-12`, `AV-6`, `AR-8`, `OP-24`, `AC-14`).
- Teardown slider, sub-assembly isolation, X-Ray and thermal overlays, fault injection, and 6 color schemes.
- WebGL post-processing pipeline with automatic 2D canvas software-renderer fallback.
